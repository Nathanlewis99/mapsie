# Mapsie partner programme: outreach pack

Start outreach in the first week of January 2027. That's when honeymoons and tours are booked and 2027 events are planned. Aim for 2–3 pilot partners before scaling up.

- **Targets:** `targets.csv`, 40 organisations with priorities. Start with the A rows.
- **Pitch PDF to attach:** `mapsie-partners.pdf`, one page.
- **Public page to link to:** https://mapsie.shop/partners. Its enquiry form emails the sales inbox.
- **Admin:** https://mapsie.shop/admin/partners, for setting partners up, making codes and invoices, and tracking commission.

## The offer (keep it simple)

| | Commission partner | Prepaid codes |
|---|---|---|
| What they do | Share their link (mapsie.shop/with/their-name) or a discount code with customers | Buy codes up front and give them away |
| What they pay | Nothing | Trade price: 15% off, 20% off for 10+, 25% off for 50+ |
| What they get | 10% of every order (print price, excluding delivery), paid monthly | Each code works like a gift card for any print |
| Good for | Tour operators, travel agents, registries | Events (finisher packs), thank-you gifts, corporate and estate agents |

Their customers can also get 10% off with a partner code. That's optional: offer it when it helps them say yes.

Margin check: the biggest discount here is 25% (trade price for 50+ codes), or 10% commission plus a 10% customer code. Before agreeing anything deeper, such as a bigger discount or free express delivery, check the live Prodigi costs on /admin/costs. Framed A4 is £69 in the UK and A3 framed is £89.

## Before the first email: make a sample

For each A target, set up their partner in admin, add their best-known route, and screenshot the finished map from mapsie.shop/with/their-name. Mark the partner "paused" until they say yes. Sending someone a map of their own route is far more convincing than a description.

## Follow-up, 5–7 working days later, in the same thread

> Hi {first name}, just bringing this back to the top of your inbox. The sample map of {route} is ready to try at {partner link}. Have a play, and if it's not for you, no problem at all.

Send one follow-up only. If there's no reply, try a different contact or channel (LinkedIn, the contact form) a month later.

## When they say yes

1. In /admin/partners, set the partner live, add their contact email and check the commission rate.
2. Add each of their routes. Use {name} and {detail} in the title or subtitle if they want finishers' names and times on the print.
3. Optional: create their customer discount code, for example OPERATORNAME10.
4. Send them their links, one per route, plus a short paragraph they can paste into emails.
5. For prepaid codes: make the batch, download the CSV, send them the codes, then click "Send Stripe invoice". Stripe emails the invoice with 14 days to pay.
6. Pay commission monthly by bank transfer, then click "Record a payment" so the amount owed resets.

## Pilot goals (January to March 2027)

- 3 partners live: 1 tour operator, 1 event and 1 corporate or registry
- 20+ partner orders, or 1 prepaid batch of 20+ codes
- Learn which offer converts best (commission or prepaid), then scale outreach to the B list
